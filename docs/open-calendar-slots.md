### Open Calendar Slots Extension

Booking links are efficient, but they feel transactional. Sometimes you want to offer someone your time, not send them to a scheduling page. Open Calendar Slots reads your Google Calendar, finds the gaps between your meetings, and gives you a clean list of times you can paste into any message. Personal scheduling, without the back-and-forth.

## How to use

1. Install the extension from the [Chrome Web Store](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/open-calendar-slots/opoakhhncnmciiocjfbgfbkpkahfhfle).
2. Click the toolbar icon, then **Sign in with Google** and grant calendar read access. A popup will appear — press allow.
3. Pick which calendars and weekdays count as "busy", set your work hours, minimum slot length, and buffer time before/after meetings.
4. Click **Find Free Time**, then **Copy to Clipboard**. Paste into any email or message.

## Example output

```
Here are some times that work for me:

Monday, 5/18: 10am-12pm, 2pm-4pm
Tuesday, 5/19: After 3pm
Wednesday, 5/20: 9am-11am, 1pm-5pm (all day)

All times in EST
```

## Privacy

Open Calendar Slots uses read-only Google Calendar access. All processing happens on your device — no event data is stored or transmitted off-device. See the [Privacy & Limited Use Policy](/open-calendar-slots/privacy/) and [Terms of Service](/open-calendar-slots/terms/) for details.
